# Kaligiri Renewable Energy Website - Design & Build Prompt

## Project Overview
Design and build a complete website for **Kaligiri Renewable Energy Private Limited** — a premium, minimalistic, modern renewable-energy and sustainability brand that appeals to farmers, investors, industrial buyers, government partners, and environmentally conscious consumers.

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: Kaligiri Renewable Energy Private Limited
- **Logo**: Use the provided logo (Untitled design.png) as primary brand mark
- **Positioning**: Trustworthy, innovative, grounded, globally relevant
- **Reference Brands**: ReNew, Azure Power, Reliance Biofuels, international biogas leaders
- **Tone**: Premium infrastructure and clean-energy — not a startup pitch deck

## Design System

### Color Palette (Earth-Inspired)
- **Primary Backgrounds**: Soft cream (`#FAF9F5`), pale green/mint (`#E8F5E9`, `#F1F8E9`), generous white space
- **Accent Colors**: Deep forest green (`#1B4D3E`) and earthy brown/terracotta (`#C86C3F`)
- **Dark Green Usage**: Navigation, footer, key CTAs primarily
- **Avoid**: Large dark sections that feel visually heavy

### Typography
- **Headings**: DM Serif Display (modern serif, premium/timeless)
- **Body**: Inter (clean sans-serif)
- **Hierarchy**: Large headings, comfortable line heights, short readable paragraphs
- **Content Reduction**: ~50% less volume while preserving core message

### Spacing & Layout
- Mobile-first, fully responsive
- Container max-width: 1200px
- Generous whitespace, clean grid systems
- Large touch targets (min 44×44px)

## Site Structure & Content

### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline**: "Clean Energy from Nature. Powered by Innovation."
- **Subheadline**: Kaligiri Renewable Energy produces Compressed Biogas (CBG) and sustainable agri-products from Super Napier Grass and biomass, supporting farmers, industry, and a lower-carbon future.
- **Primary CTA**: Explore Our Solutions
- **Secondary CTA**: Our Story
- **Visual**: Modern CBG plant illustration / Napier grass field / sustainable-energy imagery (SVG)
- **Removed**: Statistics, legacy cards, institutional mentions, excessive supporting content

### 2. Our Solutions (3 Cards)
1. **Compressed Biogas (CBG) – 12 TPD Plant** — Renewable transport and industrial fuel from Super Napier Grass and biomass
2. **Fermented Organic Manure (FOM)** — Premium organic fertilizer derived from digester slurry for sustainable agriculture
3. **Non-Torrefied Biomass Pellets** — Clean, renewable solid fuel for industrial, commercial, and domestic use
- Each: Product name, one-line description, "Learn More" action

### 3. Why Kaligiri (4 Trust Pillars)
- **Sustainable Feedstock** — Super Napier Grass and local biomass
- **Proven Technology** — Efficient 12 TPD CBG process
- **Farmer & Community Impact** — New income streams and rural jobs
- **Lower-Carbon Future** — Reduced fossil-fuel dependency
- Simple line icons, concise explanations, no emojis

### 4. Our Story (Visual Timeline)
- Founded by Young Engineers & Women Entrepreneurs
- 12 TPD CBG Plant in Kaligiri (300 tonnes Napier grass daily)
- Integrated Model — Energy + organic manure + pellets for circular sustainability
- Commitment to Farmers & Youth — 40 direct jobs, 400+ indirect livelihoods

### 5. Mission & Vision (Two-Column)
- **Mission**: Produce sustainable CBG from local feedstocks, reducing India's petroleum imports while generating direct employment for 40 people and indirect livelihoods for 400 families in Kaligiri
- **Vision**: Empower youth entrepreneurs and farmers in Andhra Pradesh through scalable renewable energy projects that enrich soil, boost crop yields, support the state's P4 Concept under CM Sri N. Chandrababu Naidu's leadership — advancing India's 500 GW non-fossil fuel capacity by 2030, SATAT-driven CBG expansion, and net-zero by 2070

### 6. Technology & Process (5 Steps)
1. Feedstock intake and preprocessing
2. Anaerobic digestion and biogas generation
3. Biogas purification and compression to CBG
4. Slurry conversion to Fermented Organic Manure
5. Residue processing into biomass pellets

### 7. Impact & Sustainability (5 Cards)

## Technical Requirements

### Performance & Accessibility
- Lighthouse scores > 95 (Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, SEO)
- Semantic HTML5
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Prefers-reduced-motion support
- High contrast mode support
- Keyboard navigation
- Focus indicators
- Skip links
- Alt text for all images

### Responsive Breakpoints
- Mobile: < 640px
- Tablet: 640px - 899px
- Desktop: 900px - 1023px
- Large: ≥ 1024px

### Animations (Subtle Only)
- Gentle fades, slight upward motion on scroll
- Soft hover effects (translateY, shadow)
- Floating animation on hero illustration (6s ease-in-out)
- No parallax, no aggressive motion

### Browser Support
- Modern browsers (last 2 versions)
- Graceful degradation
- Native lazy loading with fallback

## Content Guidelines
- Rewrite all content in clear, stakeholder-friendly language
- Short sentences, simple language, emotionally resonant storytelling
- Avoid jargon: "multi-feedstock anaerobic digestion optimization", "regulator-defensible carbon dossiers"
- Understandable within seconds by someone with no energy/agriculture background
- Replace emojis with refined icons
- Remove visual clutter, reduce shadows, simplify card designs, eliminate excessive borders

## Deliverable
A single-page, production-ready website that feels like a world-class renewable-energy and sustainability brand combining agricultural heritage, modern technology, farmer empowerment, and global climate impact in a calm, timeless, universally accessible experience.
